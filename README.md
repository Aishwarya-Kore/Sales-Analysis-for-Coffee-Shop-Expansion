# Sales-Analysis-for-Coffee-Shop-Expansion
Analyzed Monday Coffee's online sales data using SQL to identify high-potential cities for expansion in India. Evaluated revenue, sales volume, customer demand, and customer distribution to recommend the top three locations for new coffee shops and support data-driven business decisions.

## Objective
The primary objective of this project is to recommend the best cities for expanding Monday Coffee's physical store presence. By analyzing sales performance and market potential, the project identifies locations that offer strong customer demand, high revenue opportunities, and favorable operating costs.

## Tools & Technologies
* SQL
* MySQL
* Data Analysis

## Business Questions Addressed
### 1. Coffee Consumer Estimation
* Estimate the number of coffee consumers in each city, assuming 25% of the population consumes coffee.

### 2. Total Revenue Analysis
* Calculate total revenue generated from coffee sales during the last quarter of 2023.

### 3. Product Sales Performance
* Determine the total units sold for each coffee product.

### 4. Average Sales per Customer
* Calculate the average sales amount generated per customer in each city.

### 5. City Population Analysis
* Compare city populations with estimated coffee consumers.

### 6. Top-Selling Products by City
* Identify the top three best-selling coffee products in each city.

### 7. Customer Distribution Analysis
* Count unique customers purchasing coffee products in each city.

### 8. Average Sales vs. Rent Analysis
* Compare average sales per customer with average rent per customer across cities.

### 9. Monthly Sales Growth
* Analyze monthly sales trends and calculate growth or decline percentages over time.

### 10. Market Potential Assessment
* Evaluate cities based on:
  * Total Revenue
  * Customer Count
  * Estimated Coffee Consumers
  * Rental Costs
  * Sales Performance

## Key Insights

### 📈 Revenue Performance
* Pune generated the highest overall revenue among all cities.
* Several metropolitan cities showed strong customer demand and sales consistency.
* 
### 👥 Customer Demand
* Delhi recorded the highest estimated coffee consumer base.
* Jaipur and Delhi had the largest active customer counts.

### 💰 Cost Efficiency
* Pune and Jaipur demonstrated relatively low rent costs compared to their sales performance.
* Lower rent per customer indicates better profitability potential.

### 📊 Sales Trends
* Sales growth analysis revealed consistent demand for coffee products across major cities.
* A small group of products contributed significantly to overall revenue.

## Recommended Cities for Expansion
### 1. Pune
* Highest total revenue.
* High average sales per customer.
* Low rent per customer.
* Strong balance between demand and operating costs.

### 2. Delhi
* Largest estimated coffee consumer market (~7.7 million).
* Strong customer base.
* Average rent per customer remains within an acceptable range.
  
### 3. Jaipur
* Highest number of active customers.
* Very low rent per customer.
* Strong average sales per customer.
* Attractive market for cost-effective expansion.

## Conclusion
Based on sales performance, customer demand, market size, and operational costs, **Pune, Delhi, and Jaipur** emerge as the most promising cities for Monday Coffee's next phase of expansion. These locations offer the best combination of revenue potential, customer reach, and cost efficiency, making them ideal candidates for new coffee shop openings.
